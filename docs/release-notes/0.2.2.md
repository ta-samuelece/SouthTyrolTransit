## Stop names

- **Stop names in the language you choose**: a new "Stop names" setting in Settings → General shows stop, place and line names in German or Italian, separately from the app's language. Boards, the map, lines, trips, the planner, saved stops, the widget and follow notifications all use it; alerts stay in the app's language.
- Where STA's timetable has no German name, the app fills it in from the journey planner, so "Stop names: Deutsch" works offline too.
- Destinations are shown in German where they name the trip's last stop or its town ("Merano Stazione" becomes "Meran Bahnhof"); others stay in Italian rather than half translated.

## Following a ride

- **Follow a bus to your stop**: follow a bus from a stop's board and the notification says "At <stop> in N min", counting down until it leaves that stop. The stop is marked in the trip timeline and on the Android 16 Live Update bar.
- **A calmer ride page**: the line and destination sit at the top, without the date, progress bar and vehicle details, and service notices fold into one slim row.

## Notices

- **Open the full alert from a journey**: tap a notice on a journey leg to read the matching alert in full.
- Each service notice is shown once, even when STA repeats it for every day, and with its real title instead of "Service notice".
