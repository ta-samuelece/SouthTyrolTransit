## Map

- **Follow me**: tap the location button to fly to where you are and keep following you. Tap it again and the map also turns with the way you face, leaning back so you see what lies ahead; the button becomes a compass, and tapping it turns the map back north.
- Following stays on while you zoom. Dragging the map, tapping a marker or jumping to a saved stop lets go.

## Journey, stop and trip pages

- **The map sits on top of the page**: on a journey, a stop, a trip and while following, the map fills the top of the screen with the back button and the live status on it.
- Pull the handle down, or tap the map, to open it large; scroll the page and it slides over the map, and the header shows the page's title.
