## A new look for Plan and Alerts

- **Plan starts with "Where to?"**: Routing options and Settings sit at the top of the screen. The options open in place, with switches for wheelchair and bike and one-tap buttons for the rest.
- **One time button**: the departure time is a single clock button next to **Find connections**. It shows the time you chose, and its × goes back to now.
- **Service alerts for what you saved**: **Your lines** and **Your stops** cards count the alerts that affect them now and soon, and a tap shows just those. The cards appear only when something you saved is affected, and slim down while you scroll.
- Alerts are grouped into **In force now** and **Coming up**, and you can search them from the top of the screen.

## Stop boards

- **A simpler header**: the same time button as Plan, and one row for the direction that opens a sheet naming each side in full, with the lines that leave from it.
- Departures and arrivals get their own row when the stop also has directions.
- The order of planned connections is picked from the same kind of sheet.

## Search

- **Plan, the map and the stop pickers show the same results**, under the name in your language.
- Stops are found by every name they have, for example "Untermais" finds Merano Maia Bassa.
- "No matches" no longer flashes while results are loading, and scrolling the results no longer closes the search sheet.
- A stop board keeps the name you tapped, and saves the stop under it.

## Updates and settings

- **A new update screen**: highlight cards and all changes in English, German and Italian, and it stays open while the update downloads. You will see it from the next update on.
- **What's new in this version** is in Settings.
- Choices in Settings, such as language and map type, open as a sheet: tap one to apply it.
- Ropeways show the cable car icon. The offline timetable is downloaded again once for this.

## Fixes

- A trip's duration no longer wraps onto two lines on the connections list.
- **Later** now closes the "update ready" and "update failed" prompts.
