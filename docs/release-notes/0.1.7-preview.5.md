## What's new

- **Saved places**: keep Home, Work and your own places, each with an icon. Tap one to plan a trip there from where you are, or long-press it to start from it or arrive at it.
- **Follow a ride**: pick a bus on the map or open its trip and follow it. A strip and a notification keep you posted on where it is.
- **See where your bus is on the plan**: a leg of a planned trip shows the bus's position and can open the stops before yours.
- **Better trip planning**: fewer changes by default, a setting for the longest walk to a stop, trips that already left are hidden, and stations listed under two names are found.
- **More results in place search**: the planner also lists timetable stops the online search missed.
- **Recent searches** on the map search.
- **Offline timetable check**: on every start the app checks the offline timetable and offers to download it when it is missing, expired or outdated.
- **Less storage used**: installed update downloads are deleted when the app starts, and the app is a bit smaller.
