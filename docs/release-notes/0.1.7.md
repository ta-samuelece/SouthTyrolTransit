## What's new

- **New app icon**: Alpine Route, mountains and a winding route between two stops. The earlier icons are still in Settings → Appearance.
- **Saved places**: keep Home, Work and your own places, each with an icon. Tap one to plan a trip there from where you are, or long-press it to start from it or arrive at it.
- **Follow a ride**: pick a bus on the map or open its trip and follow it. A strip and a notification keep you posted on where it is.
- **See where your bus is on the plan**: a leg of a planned trip shows the bus's position and can open the stops before yours.
- **Better trip planning**: fewer changes by default, a setting for the longest walk to a stop, a **"Travelling with a bike"** option, trips that already left are hidden, and stations listed under two names are found. Place search also lists timetable stops the online search missed, and the map search remembers your recent searches.

## The map

- **Map types**: standard, detailed, aerial photos and topographic, from a new button above "My position" or in Settings → Map. Aerial and topographic cover South Tyrol.
- **Your position** shows a compass cone for the way you face and a halo for how precise it is.
- **Stop markers show their mode** in colour, stations turn into icons when you zoom in, and a red badge marks stops with a current service alert.
- **More to show**: EV charging stations (off by default), STA bike boxes, e-bike sharing, and the free-space forecast for car parks that publish one. The layers sheet groups everything by public transport, car and bike.

## Trains and bikes

- Boards and trips show **train numbers** and whether bikes are allowed; the trip screen shows how far you have come and the next stop.
- **Live train delays** show on a trip however you opened it.

## Smoother and lighter

- Smoother map, departure boards, lists and the Following screen, and smoother scrolling on stop, trip and line pages.
- On every start the app checks the offline timetable and offers to download it when it is missing, expired or outdated.
- Installed update downloads are deleted when the app starts, and the app is smaller.
- When location is refused, the map, planner and search offer a button to open the app's settings.

## Fixes

- The widget setup screen no longer opens for widgets that belong to other apps.
- Long-pressing a place card opens the Arrive here / Start from here menu again.
